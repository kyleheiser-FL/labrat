Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LabRat Peptide Tracker (“LabRat,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the LabRat web app, Android app, or related services.

Health and safety disclaimer: LabRat is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. LabRat is intended for personal tracking, educational organization, and record keeping only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use LabRat, we may collect or store the following categories of information:

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers, and account status used to create and secure your account.
  • User-provided tracking data: cycle plans, schedule entries, calculator values, notes, compounds you choose to track, order records, and related biosafety logs.
  • Health-related information you choose to provide: uploaded bloodwork files, lab values, biomarkers, notes, and generated analysis summaries. This information is only processed to provide features you request.
  • Purchase and order information: cart contents, order history, fulfillment status, and related transaction records. We do not store full payment card numbers unless handled by a separate payment provider under its own terms.
  • Technical information: device type, browser type, app version, crash or performance data, IP address, and basic usage information needed to keep the service secure and reliable.

How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, protect, and improve LabRat, including to:

  • create and authenticate accounts;
  • save your tracking, planning, calculator, order, and bloodwork records;
  • generate app features such as schedules, dashboards, analyzer outputs, and recommendations;
  • respond to support requests and account deletion requests;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
  • comply with legal obligations and platform requirements.

Health-related data

LabRat may allow you to enter or upload health-related information, such as bloodwork results and biomarker values. You control whether to provide this information. We use it only to deliver the app features you request and to store your records for your access. LabRat does not replace professional medical advice and should not be used for emergency care or clinical decision-making.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, database, authentication, analytics, security, email, payment, or order fulfillment providers. These providers are only permitted to process information for the services they provide to us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or with your consent.

Third-party services

LabRat may use third-party services including Firebase/Google Cloud services for authentication, database, hosting, and app infrastructure. Third-party service providers process information according to their own privacy and security terms.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including authenticated access and secure network connections where available. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide LabRat, maintain account records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request account or data deletion as described below.

Account deletion and data deletion

If you created an account, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by visiting Delete Account or by contacting support. After verifying the request, we will delete or de-identify associated personal data unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, or legitimate business purposes.

Your choices

  • You may choose not to provide optional tracking or health-related data.
  • You may delete or edit records inside the app where supported.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting us.

Children’s privacy

LabRat is not intended for children. The app is intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from your local laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify users, such as updating the date above or providing an in-app notice.
