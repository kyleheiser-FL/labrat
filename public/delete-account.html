Delete Account

Last updated: May 27, 2026

You can request deletion of your LabRat Peptide Tracker account and associated personal data using the instructions below.

Account deletion request: Email us from the same email address used for your LabRat account so we can verify ownership.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@labratapp.app.
  2. Use the subject line: LabRat Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your LabRat account.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

After verification, we will delete or de-identify personal data associated with your account, including account profile data, cycle records, notes, uploaded bloodwork records, analyzer history, saved calculator data, and other app records tied to your account where deletion is technically and legally possible.

What may be retained

Some records may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, chargeback, security, or compliance reasons. Retained records will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required by law or for security verification.
