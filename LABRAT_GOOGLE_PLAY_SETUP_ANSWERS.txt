Full description:
LabRat Peptide Tracker is a secure planning and record keeping app for organizing research-style peptide and compound tracking workflows. Create cycle schedules, calculate reconstitution values, manage compound notes, review bloodwork records, and keep biosafety logs in one dashboard.

Features:
• Cycle planning and schedule tracking
• Reconstitution calculator
• Compound and peptide reference organization
• Bloodwork upload and record summaries
• Personal biosafety and protocol notes
• Member shop and order records where available
• Progressive Web App install support

Information in the app is for personal tracking, education, and record keeping only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
